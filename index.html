        How to write your steps

        So your recipe works for everyone, please:

        1. Add your ingredients first. Fill in the name, amount and unit for each one.
        2. Put one step in each box. Tap “+ Add step” for the next one.
        3. Tap an ingredient’s button instead of typing its name. When a step uses an ingredient, tap that ingredient’s button under the steps. Then the amounts change on their own when someone changes the number of servings.
        Example: “Add 2 cups rice and 4 cups water” automatically becomes “4 cups rice and 8 cups water” when someone doubles it.
