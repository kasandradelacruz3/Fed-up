    Community guidelines

    Fed Up is a place for moms to share the meals they really make. Keep it kind, real and family friendly.

    • Share your own food. Post photos you took and recipes you make. Give credit if a recipe came from someone else.
    • Be kind. Every family eats differently. No put-downs, bullying or mom-shaming.
    • Keep it family friendly. Nothing you would not want your kids to see.
    • No ads, spam or selling. Fed Up is free for everyone.
    • Baby food is not medical advice. Check with your pediatrician about allergies, textures and when to start new foods.

    If you see a post that breaks these rules, tap the three dots on it and choose Report. The site owner reviews every report and can remove posts.

    Privacy

    • What other members see: your name, username, bio, the meals you post, and who you follow.
    • What only you see: your recipes (unless you share them), ingredients, weekly plan, shopping list and baby log.
    • What we keep: your email address and what you add to Fed Up, so the app works on all your devices.
    • We never sell your information or share it with advertisers.

    To have your account and everything in it deleted, contact the site owner.

    Would you like baby features?

    Baby features add a Baby food side to your meals for purées, mashes and finger foods, with age ranges, baby-friendly hashtags and a guide to what each recipe is a good source of. You also get a Baby log to record each new food your baby tries and how it went. Baby food ingredients go on the same shopping list as the rest of the family's.

    You can turn baby features on or off any time in Settings.
